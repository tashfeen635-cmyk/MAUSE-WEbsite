Technical Support Services

When technology fails, business stops. A crashed server means lost productivity. Non-functioning computers disrupt operations. Network problems halt communication. Printer issues delay critical documents. In Gilgit-Baltistan, where specialized IT support is scarce, technical problems can persist for days or weeks, causing significant business losses and frustration. Organizations often resort to trial-and-error fixes or wait for technicians from other cities.

Maqsood Ahmed & Sons provides professional technical support services that keep your systems running smoothly. Reliable technical support isn't a luxury—it's essential for business continuity, employee productivity, customer service, operational efficiency, and minimizing costly downtime. When problems occur, you need expert help quickly, not eventually.

Impact Story: From Crisis to Confidence

The Situation: A busy travel agency in Gilgit operated with 8 computers handling bookings, customer inquiries, and accounting. They had no formal IT support—when problems occurred, staff tried fixing things themselves or called various technicians. One Friday afternoon during peak tourist season, their server crashed. All booking data became inaccessible. Customers were calling for confirmations. New bookings couldn't be processed. The owner frantically searched for help, but it was weekend and most technicians were unavailable. Saturday and Sunday passed with critical business disruption. Estimated loss: PKR 500,000 in bookings and immeasurable damage to reputation.

The Solution: After this crisis, they contracted our technical support services. We provided: comprehensive system audit identifying vulnerabilities, implementation of proper backup systems, documented IT procedures and escalation paths, dedicated support hotline with guaranteed response times, remote monitoring for early problem detection, and regular maintenance preventing issues. Monthly cost: PKR 25,000—equivalent to lost revenue from just 2 hours of downtime.

The Results: Over the next 18 months, they experienced zero major outages, minor issues resolved within hours (often remotely), proactive maintenance prevented problems before impact, staff confidence increased knowing help was available, and customer service improved with reliable systems. Most importantly, the owner sleeps peacefully knowing IT problems won't spiral into crises. The travel agency expanded operations and opened a second office—confident their IT infrastructure has professional support. This is the value of reliable technical support: it's insurance against the cost of downtime, protection for your business continuity, and peace of mind in the digital age.

6. Problem Resolution

8. Follow-Up
