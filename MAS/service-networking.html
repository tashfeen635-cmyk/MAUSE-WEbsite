Networking & System Integration

Modern organizations depend on seamless connectivity and integrated systems. A hotel needs WiFi for guests, point-of-sale systems integrated with inventory management. A school requires computer labs networked for resource sharing and internet access. A government office needs secure networks connecting multiple departments. Yet in Gilgit-Baltistan, professional networking and system integration services are often unavailable locally, forcing organizations to struggle with poorly designed networks, disconnected systems, and unreliable connectivity.

Maqsood Ahmed & Sons provides professional networking and system integration services that connect people, devices, and systems efficiently and securely. Proper network infrastructure isn't just about internet access—it's about enabling collaboration, improving productivity, securing information, facilitating communication, and building the digital foundation for modern operations.

Impact Story: From Connectivity Chaos to Efficient Operations

The Problem: A growing 50-room hotel in Hunza was losing business due to poor IT infrastructure. Guests complained about unreliable WiFi, the front desk couldn't efficiently check guests in/out, accounting was manual and error-prone, and different departments operated in isolation with no information sharing. The owner had invested in computers and internet, but nothing worked smoothly together. Staff spent hours on tasks that should take minutes. Customer satisfaction scores were declining.

Our Integrated Solution: We conducted a comprehensive assessment and designed an integrated network and system solution. First, we installed a professional WiFi network with multiple access points providing seamless coverage across all guest rooms and common areas. Then we established a secure wired network connecting front desk, accounting, restaurant, and management. We integrated the property management system with accounting software and point-of-sale systems. We set up centralized backup, implemented network security including firewall and guest network isolation, and configured printing and file sharing across departments. Total investment: PKR 1.2 million.

The Transformation: Within months, the results were dramatic: WiFi became the hotel's selling point mentioned in positive reviews, check-in/check-out time reduced from 15 minutes to 3 minutes, accounting errors dropped to near zero with integrated systems, staff productivity increased significantly with shared information access, and management gained real-time visibility into operations through dashboard reports. Guest satisfaction scores increased by 35%. The hotel's TripAdvisor ranking improved from #15 to #4 in the valley. Room revenue increased 28% in the first year as repeat business and positive reviews attracted more guests. The owner realized that the PKR 1.2 million network investment paid for itself in less than 6 months through operational efficiency and increased business. This is the power of proper networking and integration—it transforms operations and directly impacts the bottom line.
