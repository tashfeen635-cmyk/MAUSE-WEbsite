Business Development Training

Business development training transforms lives in Gilgit-Baltistan by bridging the gap between entrepreneurial spirit and business success. In a region rich with opportunities but limited access to formal business education, practical training programs create pathways from ideas to thriving enterprises that strengthen local economies and create employment.

Maqsood Ahmed & Sons has witnessed firsthand how proper business training can turn a struggling venture into a successful enterprise. Our training programs are designed specifically for the GB context—addressing real challenges like limited access to markets, seasonal business cycles, infrastructure constraints, and the unique opportunities in tourism, agriculture, and local crafts.

Impact Story: From Struggling Shop to Regional Success

Meet Ayesha: A talented artisan in Hunza producing beautiful handwoven textiles, operating from a small shop that barely covered costs. Despite quality products, she struggled with pricing (often too low), had no business records, missed the tourist season peak, and couldn't expand despite demand.

The Training Journey: Ayesha joined our comprehensive Business Development Training program. Over six months, she learned: proper cost accounting and pricing strategies, basic bookkeeping and financial management, marketing and customer engagement, seasonal inventory planning, online sales platforms, and business expansion strategies. Most importantly, she learned to see her craft as a business, not just a hobby.

The Transformation: Within one year, Ayesha increased her profit margins by 120%, established an online presence reaching customers nationwide, hired three local women artisans, implemented seasonal production planning maximizing tourist season sales, and secured a partnership with a fair-trade export organization. Today, her business provides stable livelihoods for four families and has inspired five other women to formalize their craft businesses. She now mentors others in our training program—proof that business skills create ripple effects throughout communities.
