Strategic Research & Analysis

In the rapidly evolving landscape of Gilgit-Baltistan, strategic research and analysis are not just business tools—they are the difference between success and costly mistakes. Maqsood Ahmed & Sons brings over five decades of regional expertise combined with modern research methodologies to provide insights that drive informed decision-making.

Understanding the unique challenges of mountain economies, seasonal variations, infrastructure constraints, and emerging opportunities in GB requires deep local knowledge paired with rigorous analytical frameworks. Our research services transform complex regional dynamics into clear, actionable strategies.

Impact Story: The Tourism Investment Decision

The Situation: A consortium of investors was considering a major tourism development project in the Hunza Valley—a luxury resort targeting international tourists. Initial projections showed attractive returns based on growing tourism numbers.

Our Research Revealed: Through comprehensive strategic analysis, we uncovered critical insights: seasonal access limitations reduced the operational window to 6 months, international tourist demographics showed preference for authentic cultural experiences over luxury isolation, emerging competition from boutique local properties, and infrastructure constraints affecting supply chains. Most critically, we identified an untapped domestic tourism market segment seeking premium experiences.

The Outcome: Based on our research, the project was redesigned as a year-round cultural tourism hub with winter activities, targeting both domestic and international markets. The revised strategy reduced investment by 30%, increased projected occupancy from 45% to 72%, and created stronger community partnerships. Five years later, the resort is profitable and has spawned three successful spinoff ventures—all because strategic research revealed the real opportunity.
