Machinery Rental Services

Our machinery rental service provides access to professional construction equipment for your projects in Gilgit-Baltistan. All equipment is well-maintained, regularly serviced, and ready for immediate deployment.

Available Equipment:

  • Tractors - Agricultural and construction tractors for various applications
  • Road Rollers - Compaction equipment for road construction and soil compaction
  • Excavators - Earthmoving and excavation machinery for digging and material handling
  • Bulldozers - Heavy-duty earth-moving equipment for grading and leveling
  • Loaders - Material handling and loading equipment
  • Graders - Motor graders for road surface preparation
  • Compactors - Soil and asphalt compaction equipment

Equipment Specifications:

  • Various sizes and capacities available
  • Modern, well-maintained fleet
  • Regular servicing and safety checks
  • Fuel-efficient models
  • Suitable for mountain terrain
