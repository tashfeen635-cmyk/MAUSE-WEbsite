IT Solutions for Businesses

In Gilgit-Baltistan's competitive business environment, technology is no longer optional—it's essential for survival and growth. Yet many businesses struggle with fragmented IT: computers from different vendors, incompatible software, unreliable networks, no proper support, inadequate security, and technology decisions made without strategic planning. This piecemeal approach leads to inefficiency, vulnerability, and missed opportunities.

Maqsood Ahmed & Sons provides complete IT solutions designed specifically for businesses in Gilgit-Baltistan. We don't just sell hardware or fix problems—we become your IT partner, understanding your business objectives and delivering technology solutions that drive efficiency, enable growth, protect your assets, and provide competitive advantages.

Impact Story: From IT Chaos to Strategic Advantage

The Challenge: A chain of three retail stores in Gilgit-Baltistan was growing but struggling with technology. Each store had different POS systems that didn't communicate, inventory management was manual with frequent errors, the owner had no real-time visibility into operations, staff used personal phones and WhatsApp for business communication, customer data was scattered across notebooks and spreadsheets, and accounting was a monthly nightmare. Despite investing in technology, it created more problems than it solved. The owner was tech-frustrated and considering abandoning computerization.

Our Integrated Solution: We conducted a comprehensive business analysis and designed a complete IT solution: centralized inventory management system synchronized across all three stores, unified POS system with real-time data to headquarters, secure WiFi networks at each location, business phone system with proper extensions, cloud-based customer relationship management, integrated accounting system, owner's dashboard providing real-time business metrics, automated backup protecting all business data, and complete IT support and maintenance. We implemented in phases over 3 months to minimize disruption.

The Transformation: Within 6 months, the results were remarkable: inventory accuracy improved from 70% to 98%, stock-outs reduced by 65% increasing sales, accounting close time reduced from 5 days to 1 day, the owner gained real-time visibility into all operations from anywhere, customer service improved with complete purchase history, staff productivity increased by 30%, data-driven decisions replaced guesswork, and the business expanded confidently to a fourth location. Total IT investment: PKR 1.8 million. First-year benefits: PKR 3.2 million in reduced costs and increased revenue. The owner said: "I went from hating technology to realizing it's my competitive weapon. MAS didn't just provide IT services—they transformed how my business operates." This is the power of strategic IT solutions: technology aligned with business objectives delivering measurable results.
