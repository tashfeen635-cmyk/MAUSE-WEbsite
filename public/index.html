About Us - Over 50 Years of Excellence

M/S Maqsood Ahmed & Sons started its small scale operations humbly in 1972 and today it is one of the leading construction companies of the GB region. This has become possible only with determined efforts of our dedicated staff and encouragement from our valued clients.

“Statement of Facts”

Muhammad Nabi Dar, son of Sartol Dar, is a permanent resident of Sartoli Haet, Majini Mohallah, and is presently residing and conducting business at Dar Plaza, NLI Market, Nabi Bazar, Gilgit. The land situated from Nabi Bazar, NLI Market up to Ramzan Hotel forms part of his ancestral and hometown property. Nabi Bazar is a historically recognized bazaar of Gilgit, established in the year 1945 during the administration of the then Political Agent, Colonel Cobb.
